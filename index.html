How to subscribe on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap Subscribe. Confirm the dialog.
  2. Prefer doing it by hand? Tap Copy link, then go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Add Account › Other › Add Subscribed Calendar and paste it.
  3. Give it an alert if you like, and turn Remove Alarms on to keep it quiet.

On macOS: Calendar › File › New Calendar Subscription. In Google Calendar: Other calendars › From URL. Subscribed calendars stay up to date on their own — the feed asks to be refreshed weekly.

Dates are computed from the fixed holidays plus the Easter cycle, and checked against holidays-info.com. No tracking, no server, no dependencies.
